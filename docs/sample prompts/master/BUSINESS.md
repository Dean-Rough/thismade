# BUSINESS — Business Context

## Overview
- **Name**: shopface
- **Description**: a “website before the sales pitch” service for Scottish small businesses. Find companies with weak sites, turn verified public facts into polished fictional or prospect-specific Astro previews, and let a human check every claim, image and layout before sending one private link. Previews must be unindexed, clearly unofficial, free of live forms and automatically removed after outreach expires.
Sell the accepted site as Website + Care: £39/month, with £29/month for the first ten customers, a three-month initial term and chargeable content changes. Recreate it as a contract-driven template engine with sector presets, immutable content snapshots, Vercel deployment, a small control plane, automated safety/quality checks, a public marketing site and reviewable email templates—keeping customer editing, dashboards and heavyweight automation outside the pilot.
